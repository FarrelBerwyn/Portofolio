I CAN! – Confidence Building Game

"I CAN!" is a game-based application that focuses on self-development, specifically building confidence when socializing in public and community settings. This application is inspired by personal experiences in overcoming anxiety through step-by-step exercises presented in an interactive and fun way.

4 Level

  • Personalizing – Users are invited to get to know themselves, understanding their strengths and areas for development as a basic foundation.
  • Learning – Users learn practical techniques for increasing self-confidence through an easy-to-understand approach.
  • Aspiring – Users are trained to build a positive mindset by imagining success in social situations.
  • Networking – Users practice their communication skills through realistic social interaction simulations.

Tech Stack
