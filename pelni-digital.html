PELNI Digital – Smart Maritime Ticketing & Services App

PELNI Digital is a mobile application designed to improve the accessibility and efficiency of maritime transportation services in Indonesia. As an archipelagic nation, Indonesia relies heavily on inter-island connectivity to support public mobility and economic growth. However, limited access to maritime transportation services, particularly in terms of ticket bookings and travel information, remains a challenge for many.
