Senyuman Kids Fashion kids Brand

Senyuman Kids is an Indonesian children's apparel brand and digital commerce platform designed to deliver premium quality, playful aesthetics, and maximum daily comfort for growing kids.

Concept & Brand Vision

Combining joyful, cheerful design systems with eco-friendly fabrics and an effortless mobile shopping experience. The platform features an interactive lookbook, smart size advisors, and seamless one-click order fulfillment.

Brand & Tech Development

Architected and built by Farrel Berwyn, orchestrating end-to-end digital identity, UI/UX design, high-converting product showcases, and fast e-commerce integration.

Open Senyuman Live Web

Brand Innovations & Digital Features

Key digital touchpoints developed for the Senyuman Kids omnichannel ecosystem:

01

Interactive Lookbook & Catalog

Visual-rich apparel collections with high-resolution fabric zooms, seasonal mix-and-match previews, and instant filter tags based on age and occasion.

02

Smart Fit & Size Recommender

Intelligent sizing engine that calculates optimal fit for children across age, height, and weight metrics, dramatically reducing return rates.

03

Frictionless Mobile Checkout

Optimized 2-step checkout flow supporting QRIS, e-wallets, bank transfers, and automated WhatsApp order confirmation and courier tracking.

04

Omnichannel Community & Socials

Seamlessly synced with Instagram Shop, TikTok Shop, and loyalty rewards to empower a thriving parent community.

Brand & Platform Specs

  • Category: Kids Apparel / E-Commerce / Digital Lifestyle Brand.
  • Performance: Sub-second page loads with edge CDN image caching.
  • Engagement: +45% average session duration and strong social conversion.
